## Full description (en-US)

```
Which ward am I in? Tapaiko Ward answers it in one tap.

Tap anywhere on the map of Nepal — or use your location — to instantly see the ward number, municipality or rural municipality, district and province of that spot.

FEATURES
• Find my ward: uses your phone's location to show the ward you are standing in.
• Tap to identify: tap any spot on the map to see its ward, municipality, district and province.
• Search all 753 local levels: metropolitan and sub-metropolitan cities, municipalities and rural municipalities. Type a ward number too, e.g. "Pokhara 8".
• Browse Nepal: province → district → local level → ward, with clear boundaries and labels.
• Population: ward and municipality population from the 2021 National Census.
• Share, copy or get directions to a ward.
• Works offline: areas you open are saved on your phone.
• English and नेपाली, light and dark themes, satellite and terrain maps.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your location is used only on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

DATA SOURCES
• Administrative boundaries: Survey Department (Napi Bibhag), Government of Nepal — https://dos.gov.np (served via https://maps.sandeepkafle.com.np)
• Population: National Population and Housing Census 2021, National Statistics Office, Nepal — https://censusnepal.cbs.gov.np
• Base maps: © OpenStreetMap contributors (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), OpenTopoMap, Esri

DISCLAIMER
Tapaiko Ward is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing the Government of Nepal or any government entity. Boundaries and population figures come from public sources and are for reference only; they are not an official survey or legal record.
```
